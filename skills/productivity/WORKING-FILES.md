# Disposable working files

Use working files whenever they help the assigned investigation, planning,
verification, or result transport. They need no separate approval within the
accepted task and available tool permissions. Use uniquely named files in the
OS temporary directory or a verified Git-ignored `.scratch/` directory. If
ignoring cannot be established without an unauthorized repository change, use
the OS temporary directory instead. Keep private material private and working
files out of commits.

These files are disposable working memory: cleanup may remove them. Keep an
active transport file available until its consumer has read it, but do not treat
its location as durable storage or authority. Before wrapping up, deliver useful
lasting findings and decisions through the task's authorized result channel or
durable destination. Disposable notes need no permanent copy.

A read-only assignment protects the inspected sources, candidate, tracker, and
other task targets; it does not prohibit the agent's own disposable working
files. Working files do not authorize implementation or other operational
changes. Preserve isolation: a pass may write notes from its permitted inputs,
but may not use scratch to obtain another pass's private context or give it
inputs outside the agreed contract.

Working notes are distinct from a requested deliverable. Writing them neither
selects a destination nor authorizes publication. A read-only leaf returns its
findings to the responsible caller, who owns any durable recording.
